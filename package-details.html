Day 1: Arrival at Malé International Airport. Transfer to resort by speedboat. Check-in and welcome refreshments. Evening sunset cruise.

Day 2: Snorkeling trip to reef. Water sports activities. Sunset fishing excursion.

Day 3: Spa morning. Island hopping tour. Farewell dinner on the beach.

Day 4: Breakfast. Check-out and transfer back to airport.

Pickup from Malé International Airport (MLE). Our representative will be holding a placard with your name. Please carry a printed copy of your booking confirmation. Speedboat transfer to the resort takes approximately 45 minutes.

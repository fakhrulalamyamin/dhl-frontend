Who We Are

ABOUT US

Discover Holidays Limited is a Dhaka-based travel company specializing in group tours, corporate travel, air ticketing, visa processing and Umrah packages. We are a team of experienced professionals dedicated to creating unforgettable travel experiences for our clients across 100+ destinations worldwide.

Our commitment to excellence, attention to detail, and personalized service have made us a trusted partner for thousands of travelers. We believe that every journey should be unique and memorable, which is why we tailor each itinerary to match our clients' needs and preferences.

With over a decade of experience in the travel industry, we combine local expertise with international standards to deliver world-class service at competitive prices. Our vision is to become the leading travel company in South Asia by continuously innovating and exceeding customer expectations.

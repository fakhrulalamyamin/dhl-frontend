Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tour package? +
You can book a tour package by filling out the quote request form above, calling our Tour Packages line at 01977-242404, or visiting our office in Mohakhali, Dhaka. Our team will get back to you within 24 hours.
Do you offer customized tour packages? +
Yes, we specialize in fully customized itineraries for individuals, families, and corporate groups. Simply mention your preferred destinations, travel dates, group size, and budget in the message field, and our travel experts will design your perfect trip.
How long does visa processing take? +
Visa processing times vary by country, typically ranging from 3 to 15 working days. For urgent requirements, we offer expedited processing for select destinations. Contact our Visa Services team at 01730-716619 for details.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept bank transfers, bKash, Nagad, credit/debit cards, and cash payments at our office. A booking confirmation is issued upon receipt of the agreed deposit. Full payment schedules depend on the package.
Can I modify or cancel my booking? +
Modifications and cancellations are subject to our Refund Policy and the terms of any airline or hotel bookings involved. We recommend contacting us as early as possible. Please refer to our Refund Policy page for full details.
Do you handle Umrah and Hajj packages? +
Yes, we offer complete Umrah packages including flights, accommodation in Makkah and Madinah, ziyarah tours, and visa processing. Contact our Umrah Services team at 01329-680390 for the latest packages and availability.
